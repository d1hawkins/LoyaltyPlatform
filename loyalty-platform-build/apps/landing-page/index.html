Daiso Rewards Benefits

Everything That Makes Daiso Rewards Special

Earn points on every Daiso purchase, unlock exclusive tiers, redeem rewards, and enjoy members-only offers across all Daiso USA locations.

Earn Points

Earn 1 point for every $1 spent at any Daiso USA store. Bonus points on seasonal promotions and special product categories. Full transaction enrichment: store, associate, and source tracking on every earn.

Unlock Tiers

Level up from Bronze to Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Higher tiers earn more points per dollar and unlock exclusive member benefits.

Exclusive Offers

Members-only deals on your favorite Daiso products. BOGO offers, percentage discounts, and special rewards tailored to your shopping habits.

Shop Anywhere

Earn and redeem at any Daiso USA location, in-store or online. Full transaction traceability — store ID, register, associate, and source system tracked on every purchase.

Finance Reports & Analytics

Points liability (ASC 606), breakage estimates, redemption reserves, revenue attribution, engagement funnels, and at-risk member detection — all built in.

Birthday Rewards

Get a special birthday bonus every year. Double points during your birthday month and a surprise gift waiting at your local Daiso.

Admin Portal

Comprehensive reports: finance liability, marketing funnels, operations dashboards. Redeem rewards on any Daiso product across all categories.

Secure & Private

Your personal information is protected with industry-leading security. CCPA compliant with full control over your data.

Marketing Integrations

Sync members to ActiveCampaign and Klaviyo. Trigger automations on tier changes, points earned, and more. Drive win-back campaigns and measure loyalty ROI with built-in marketing tools.

For Developers

Powered by a Modern API

The Daiso Rewards platform is built API-first. Integrate with our POS systems, e-commerce, and mobile apps through a clean REST API and JavaScript SDK.

API Docs Swagger UI POS Demo GitHub
terminal 
# Enroll a new member
curl -X POST https://api.loyaltyplatform.io/members \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  -d '{
    "email": "jane@example.com",
    "first_name": "Jane",
    "last_name": "Smith"
  }'

# Record a transaction with enrichment
curl -X POST https://api.loyaltyplatform.io/v1/transactions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  -d '{
    "memberId": "mem_abc123",
    "amount": 4500,
    "storeId": "DAISO-042",
    "associateId": "EMP-1001",
    "sourceChannel": "in-store",
    "orderRef": "ORD-2026-001"
  }'

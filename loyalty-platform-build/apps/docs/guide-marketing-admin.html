1. Understanding the Loyalty Funnel

The loyalty funnel tracks how customers progress through the program. Understanding each stage helps you identify where to focus your campaigns.

StageDefinitionKey MetricCampaign Goal
Enrollment Customer creates a loyalty account New members per week Drive sign-ups at checkout, online, and via marketing
First Purchase New member makes their first transaction with the loyalty card Activation rate (% of new members who transact within 30 days) Welcome offer, first-purchase bonus
Repeat Purchase Member returns for a second or third purchase 30-day repeat rate Visit-based punch cards, category promotions
Tier Upgrade Member earns enough points to move up a tier Upgrade rate per month Tier-progress emails, "almost there" nudges
Redemption Member redeems points or an offer Redemption rate Offer awareness, expiry reminders
Advocacy Member refers friends or shares on social media Referral count Referral bonus offers
Where to Find This
The engagement funnel is visualized in Admin Portal → Reports → Marketing tab. Each stage shows the conversion rate to the next stage, so you can quickly spot drop-off points.

2. Creating Offers for Campaigns

Percentage Discounts for Seasonal Sales

Use percentage discounts for broad seasonal campaigns (Back to School, Holiday, Spring Cleaning).

  1. Navigate to Offers → Create Offer
  2. Select type: Percentage Discount
  3. Set discount: e.g., 15%
  4. Set minimum purchase: e.g., $10.00
  5. Optional: Set a maximum discount cap (e.g., $5 max discount)
  6. Set date range to match your campaign period
  7. Set eligibility: All tiers, all channels
  8. Click Publish

Visit-Based Punch Cards

Visit-based offers are the most effective tool for driving purchase frequency. They work like a digital punch card.

  1. Select type: Visit-Based
  2. Set visits required: e.g., 5
  3. Set minimum spend per visit: e.g., $5.00
  4. Set reward: e.g., $5.00 off or 500 bonus points
  5. Optional: Restrict to specific stores or product categories
  6. Click Publish

Members see their progress toward the offer in the Member Portal and at checkout on the Self-Checkout kiosk.

Threshold Rewards

Encourage higher spending by rewarding members who reach a spending threshold within a time period.

  1. Select type: Threshold Reward
  2. Set threshold: e.g., $200 spent in a calendar month
  3. Set reward: e.g., 500 bonus points
  4. Set the evaluation period (monthly, quarterly)
  5. Click Publish

Targeting by Tier, Channel, and Store

Every offer supports targeting filters:

FilterOptionsUse Case
TierAny combination of Bronze, Silver, Gold, PlatinumExclusive Gold/Platinum offers to reward top members
ChannelIn-store, Online, AllOnline-only flash sales, in-store weekend specials
StoreAll stores, or select specific locationsGrand opening offers for new stores, store-specific clearance
Enrollment ChannelPOS, Web, Kiosk, E-CommerceTarget members based on how they joined

3. Promotional Multipliers

Double Points Events

Double (or triple) points events are a powerful way to drive traffic during specific periods.

  1. Navigate to Program Config → Promotional Multipliers
  2. Click Add Multiplier
  3. Name it: e.g., "Double Points Weekend"
  4. Set multiplier value: 2
  5. Set start and end dates/times
  6. Select applicable stores (all or specific)
  7. Click Save

The multiplier activates and deactivates automatically. No manual intervention needed at start/end time.

Category Bonuses

Award extra points for specific product categories to drive sales in targeted departments:

  • Navigate to Program Config → Category Multipliers
  • Set multipliers for each category:
    • Beauty & Cosmetics: 3x
    • Kitchen & Dining: 2x
    • Seasonal Items: 2.5x
    • Stationery: 1.5x
  • Category multipliers are always active until you change them

Store-Specific Promotions

Running a grand opening or want to boost traffic at an underperforming location? Create a store-specific multiplier:

  • Same process as a double points event, but select only the target store(s)
  • Combine with a store-specific offer for maximum impact
  • Example: "3x points at our new Carrollton location for the grand opening week"

4. Marketing Integrations

Connecting ActiveCampaign

  1. Navigate to Integrations in the Admin Portal sidebar
  2. Find ActiveCampaign and click Connect
  3. Enter your ActiveCampaign API URL (e.g., https://yourcompany.api-us1.com)
  4. Enter your ActiveCampaign API key (found in Settings → Developer)
  5. Click Test Connection to verify
  6. Select which fields to sync:
    • Member ID
    • Email and phone
    • Current tier name
    • Points balance
    • Enrollment date
    • Last transaction date
    • Member status
  7. Click Enable Sync

Once connected, member data syncs automatically. New enrollments create contacts in ActiveCampaign. Tier changes and point balance updates refresh the contact fields.

Connecting Klaviyo

  1. Navigate to Integrations and click Connect next to Klaviyo
  2. Enter your Klaviyo Private API Key (found in Account → Settings → API Keys)
  3. Click Test Connection
  4. Map loyalty fields to Klaviyo profile properties
  5. Click Enable Sync

Setting Up Automation Triggers

Use loyalty events to trigger marketing automation flows in ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo:

EventAutomation Idea
member.enrolledWelcome Series: 3-email sequence introducing the program, benefits, and first-purchase offer
tier.upgradedTier Celebration: Congratulations email with new benefits and an exclusive offer
points.expiringExpiry Warning: Reminder at 30 days and 7 days before expiry with a CTA to shop
No purchase in 60 daysWin-Back Campaign: "We miss you" email with a bonus points offer
transaction.completedPost-Purchase: Thank you email with points balance update and next-tier progress

Custom Fields Synced

The following fields are synced to your marketing platform as custom properties:

  • loyalty_tier — Current tier name (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)
  • loyalty_points — Current points balance
  • loyalty_member_id — Unique member ID
  • loyalty_status — active, suspended, or closed
  • loyalty_enrolled_date — When they joined
  • loyalty_last_transaction — Date of most recent purchase
  • loyalty_lifetime_points — Total points ever earned

Using Merge Tags in Email Templates

Reference these synced fields in your email templates:

  • ActiveCampaign: %LOYALTY_TIER%, %LOYALTY_POINTS%
  • Klaviyo: {{ person.loyalty_tier }}, {{ person.loyalty_points }}

Example: "Hi {{ first_name }}, you're a {{ person.loyalty_tier }} member with {{ person.loyalty_points }} points. You're only 200 points away from Gold!"

5. Reports for Marketing

Navigate to Reports → Marketing tab in the Admin Portal.

Engagement Funnel

The engagement funnel shows conversion rates between each stage of the loyalty journey:

  • Enrolled → First Purchase: What percentage of new members make a purchase within 30 days?
  • First Purchase → Repeat: What percentage come back for a second purchase?
  • Repeat → Tier Upgrade: What percentage earn enough to move up a tier?
  • Active → Redemption: What percentage of active members redeem offers or points?

Low conversion at any stage indicates where your campaigns should focus. For example, a low first-purchase rate means your welcome offer needs improvement.

At-Risk Members

Members who haven't transacted in 60+ days are flagged as "at-risk." This report shows:

  • Total at-risk members by tier
  • Points balance at risk (points these members hold)
  • Last transaction date distribution

Click Export to download the at-risk member list as a CSV. Import this into ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo to run a targeted win-back campaign.

Tier Distribution

A bar chart showing how many members are in each tier. A healthy program has a pyramid shape — many Bronze, fewer Silver, even fewer Gold, and a small Platinum segment. If the distribution is flat, your tier thresholds may be too low.

Offer Performance

For each offer, view:

MetricWhat It Tells You
Redemption Rate% of issued offers actually used — below 5% means the offer isn't compelling enough
Total Discount GivenThe cost of the offer to the business
Average Basket SizeWhether the offer drives incremental spend
Revenue AttributedTotal revenue from transactions where this offer was applied
ROIRevenue attributed divided by total discount given

Cohort Retention

Track how members enrolled in a given month behave over time:

  • Month 1 retention: % of enrollees who return within 30 days
  • Month 3 retention: % still active after 90 days
  • Month 6 retention: % still active after 180 days

Use this to measure the long-term impact of program changes. If you changed the welcome offer in March, compare March cohort retention against February.

6. Per-Visit vs Per-Dollar Guidance

The platform supports two earn modes. As a marketing admin, you may be asked to recommend which mode suits the merchant best.

FactorPer-DollarPer-Visit
Average transaction value Good when baskets vary widely ($10 – $100+) Ideal when most baskets are similar ($5 – $20)
Customer motivation Encourages spending more per visit Encourages visiting more often
Simplicity Members must calculate points from receipt total "Earn 10 points every time you visit" — instant understanding
Financial predictability Liability scales with revenue Liability is flat per visit — easier to forecast
Recommended for Daiso — Per-visit is recommended. Most Daiso transactions are $5–$15 and the goal is to increase visit frequency.
Recommendation for Daiso

Use per-visit mode with a $5 minimum spend threshold and 10 points per visit. This keeps the program simple for customers ("visit and earn"), drives frequency over basket size, and gives finance a predictable cost per acquisition.

7. Visit-Based Campaign Offers

Visit-based offers are the most powerful tool for driving repeat traffic. They create a "punch card" mechanic that customers intuitively understand.

Campaign Examples

CampaignConfigurationGoal
5 Visits = $5 Off Visits: 5, Min spend: $5, Reward: $5 fixed, All stores Drive repeat visits for all members
3 Visits This Week = Free Item Visits: 3, Min spend: $3, Reward: Free item (max $5), Date range: 7 days Short-term traffic burst
10 Visits = 500 Bonus Points Visits: 10, Min spend: $5, Reward: 500 points, Per-day counting Long-term engagement for tier progression
Grand Opening: 3 Visits = $10 Off Visits: 3, Min spend: $5, Reward: $10 fixed, Store: New location only Drive traffic to a new store

Qualification Rules

When creating visit-based campaign offers, configure these qualification rules to control exactly what counts as a visit:

  • Minimum Spend: Set the floor (e.g., $5). Transactions below this amount do not count toward visit progress.
  • Minimum Items: Optionally require a minimum number of items per transaction.
  • Channels: Restrict to in-store, online, self-checkout, or any combination.
  • Stores: All stores or specific locations.
  • Counting Mode: Choose Per-Transaction (every qualifying transaction counts) or Per-Day (only the first qualifying transaction per day counts). Per-day mode prevents gaming by making multiple small purchases in a single visit.

8. Visit Analytics

Navigate to Reports → Operations tab to access visit analytics.

Key Metrics

MetricWhat It Tells YouAction
Qualified Visits Number of transactions that met the minimum spend threshold If low compared to total transactions, consider lowering the threshold
Visit Conversion Rate % of total transactions that qualify as visits Target 70–85%. Below 60% means your threshold is too high. Above 95% means it's too low to be meaningful.
Daily Visit Trend Chart of qualified visits per day over the past 30/60/90 days Spot the impact of promotions, seasonal trends, and campaign performance

Optimizing the Minimum Spend Threshold

Use the visit conversion rate to tune the minimum spend threshold:

  1. Pull the current visit conversion rate from the Operations report
  2. If it's below 60%, the threshold is excluding too many transactions. Lower it by $1–$2 increments.
  3. If it's above 95%, the threshold isn't differentiating behavior. Raise it by $1–$2 increments.
  4. The sweet spot is 70–85% — most transactions qualify, but small impulse purchases don't.
  5. After adjusting, monitor the daily visit trend for 1–2 weeks to confirm the impact.

10. At-Risk Members & Visit Data

Visit data significantly improves at-risk detection. In per-visit mode, the system tracks not just "when did the member last transact" but also "how has their visit frequency changed over time."

Enhanced At-Risk Signals

  • No transactions in 60+ days: Standard inactivity flag (works in both earn modes)
  • Visit frequency decline: In per-visit mode, if a member's rolling 30-day visit count drops below 50% of their historical average, they're flagged as "declining."
  • Unqualified visits: If a member is transacting but consistently below the minimum spend threshold, they're not earning points and may feel disengaged.

Win-Back Strategy with Visit Data

  1. Export the at-risk member list from Reports → Marketing → At-Risk Members
  2. Segment by visit frequency decline vs total inactivity
  3. For declining members: send a visit-based offer ("Complete 3 visits this month, get $5 off") to re-establish the habit
  4. For inactive members: send a stronger incentive ("We miss you! $5 off your next visit, no minimum")
  5. Import into Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign for automated flow delivery

11. Best Practices

Set points expiry to 12 months minimum

Short expiry windows (3–6 months) frustrate customers and increase churn. Industry benchmarks show 12–18 months as the sweet spot — long enough that members feel safe accumulating, short enough to drive a sense of urgency.

Visit-based offers drive frequency better than points-only programs

A "5 visits = $5 off" punch card is more intuitive and motivating than "earn 500 points for a reward." Members can easily track their progress and it encourages them to come back specifically to advance their punch card.

Make tier benefits feel meaningful

Don't just increase the earn rate at each tier. Add tangible benefits: early access to sales, exclusive products, birthday rewards, free shipping, or VIP event invitations. Members should feel a real difference when they advance.

Send points-expiry warnings at 30 and 7 days

Expiry reminder emails recover 15–20% of at-risk points. Members often didn't realize they had points about to expire. A simple "You have 350 points expiring in 7 days" with a link to the member portal drives redemption visits.

Test one variable at a time

When running promotions, change one variable (discount amount, duration, target tier) and compare results against the previous period. Running multiple changes simultaneously makes it impossible to know what worked.

Use category multipliers strategically

Apply higher multipliers to categories with good margins or excess inventory, not your best sellers. The goal is to influence behavior, not reward behavior that would happen anyway.

Review the at-risk report weekly

Catching lapsed members early (60 days) is far more effective than trying to win them back after 6 months. Set a weekly calendar reminder to export the at-risk list and queue a win-back campaign.

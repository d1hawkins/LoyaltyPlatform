2. Demo Flow (30 min)

Act 1 — "The Customer Experience" (10 min)

Start with the customer-facing applications. The goal is to show executives exactly what their shoppers will see and feel.

Self-Checkout Kiosk

Step 1: Switch to the Self-Checkout tab. Show the idle welcome screen.

Say: "This is what your customers see at the self-checkout kiosk. It's branded with Daiso colors and runs on any tablet or touchscreen."

Step 2: Tap Start → Enter phone number 9192474109 → Show the member greeting with tier badge and points balance.

Point out the personalized greeting, the member's current tier, and their points balance displayed prominently.

Step 3: Add 3–4 Daiso products to the cart. Use recognizable items (storage boxes, kitchen tools, beauty products).
Step 4: Show the offer that appears automatically. Say: "Visit-based offers unlock automatically based on how often the customer shops."
Step 5: Apply the offer → Tap PAY NOW → Show the points-earned animation.
"Every transaction earns points and tracks what they bought, where they bought it, and who served them. This data powers your marketing and finance reporting."

Coupon Security (Code-Bound Coupons)

Step 5b: Highlight the coupon code shown on the offer card. Say: "Coupon codes are unique per member and single-use. Even if a code is shared on social media, it can only be redeemed by the member who earned it."
Demo: Show the code from the SMS notification, then show it displayed on the POS offer panel. Explain that attempting to use another member's code will result in a rejection with error CODE_MEMBER_MISMATCH.
"This prevents coupon fraud. Each visit-milestone coupon is cryptographically tied to the member who earned it. No sharing, no reuse."

Member Portal

Step 6: Switch to the Member Portal tab. Log in with the same phone number (9192474109).
Step 7: Walk through each section:
  • Dashboard: Points balance, tier progress bar, tier benefits summary
  • Transactions: "The customer sees every purchase with items, store, and date"
  • Offers: "Personalized offers based on their tier and visit history"
  • Profile: "They can manage their own account — GDPR compliant"
"This is what your customers see on their phone. No app download needed — it's a responsive web app that works on any device."

Customer Enrollment

Step 8: Click "Join Now" → Fill out the enrollment form → Show instant enrollment confirmation.
"New members can join at checkout, online, or via a QR code at the register. Enrollment takes less than 30 seconds."

Act 2 — "The Merchant Experience" (10 min)

Now show the back-office tools. This is where Daiso's team manages the program day-to-day.

Step 1: Switch to the Admin Portal. Start at the Dashboard.

Say: "This is your real-time command center. You see active members, points issued today, transactions, program health at a glance, and the current earn mode badge (Per-Dollar or Per-Visit). When in per-visit mode, you also see qualified visits today and the visit conversion rate."

Step 2: Navigate to Members. Search for the test member. Click to open their detail view.

Show: full profile, transaction history, tier status, points balance, and the ability to adjust points manually.

Step 3: Navigate to Transactions. Show the list with store, associate, items, and points columns.

Say: "Every transaction is recorded with full context — store, register, associate, line items, and points earned."

Step 4: Navigate to Tiers. Show configurable tier names, thresholds, and benefits.

Say: "You can rename tiers, change point thresholds, and add or remove benefits at any time. Changes take effect immediately."

Step 5: Navigate to Offers → Create Offer. Demo creating a visit-based offer.

Example: "Buy 5 visits at $5+ each, get $5 off." Walk through the form fields: offer type, qualification rules (minimum spend, minimum items, qualifying channels, qualifying stores, per-transaction vs per-day counting), reward amount, and eligibility criteria.

Step 5b: Also show the Free Item offer type. Create an example: "Free storage bin after 3 visits." Show the optional SKU restriction and optional max value cap.

Say: "Your marketing team can create a new offer in 30 seconds — no engineering needed. You've got visit-based punch cards, percentage off, fixed amount, BOGO, threshold rewards, and free item giveaways."

Step 6: Navigate to Program Config. Show each tab:
  • Earn Mode Toggle: Switch between Per-Dollar (1 point per $1) and Per-Visit (flat points per qualifying visit). When Per-Visit is selected, show the conditional fields: points per visit, minimum spend threshold, and max visits per day.
  • Earn Rate: Points per dollar or points per visit, rounding rules
  • Promotional Multipliers: "Double points weekend — set it up in 2 clicks"
  • Category Multipliers: "3x points on beauty products"
  • Expiry: Points expiration window and notification settings

Say: "Per-visit mode is designed for high-frequency, low-ticket retailers like Daiso. Every visit over $5 earns 10 points, regardless of the total. It keeps it simple for customers and predictable for finance."

Step 7: Navigate to Reports. Show all three tabs:
  • Finance: Points Liability card, breakage estimate, redemption reserve, revenue attribution
  • Marketing: Engagement funnel, at-risk members, tier distribution chart, offer performance
  • Operations: Qualified visits today, visit conversion rate (% of transactions that meet the minimum spend), daily visit trends chart, program health score

Say: "Finance gets ASC 606 compliant liability numbers. Marketing sees the full engagement funnel. Operations monitors visit conversion rates. No manual spreadsheets."

Step 8: Navigate to Integrations. Show ActiveCampaign and Klaviyo connectors.

Say: "Connect your email platform and sync member data automatically. Trigger campaigns on loyalty events like tier upgrades or points about to expire."

Step 9: Briefly show API Keys and Audit Log.

Say: "POS integration uses API keys you generate here. And every admin action is logged — who did what, when, with full before/after snapshots."

Act 3 — "The POS Experience" (5 min)

Demonstrate the point-of-sale integration to show how associates interact with the loyalty program during checkout.

Step 1: Switch to the POS Terminal tab. Point out the store settings panel in the header — store ID, store name, register ID. Show the associate selector where the cashier picks their name. Explain that these fields are automatically attached to every transaction as enrichment data.
Step 2: Look up the test member by phone number. Show the member panel with:
  • Offers (collapsible section) with visit progress bars: "3 of 5 visits — 2 more to unlock!"
  • Recent Activity (collapsible section) showing recent transactions with store and date
Step 3: Add items to the cart → Apply an offer → Complete the sale.
Step 4: Show the receipt with points earned and updated balance. Point out that the transaction response includes all enrichment fields: storeId, storeName, registerId, associateId, associateName, sourceChannel, sourceSystem, and orderRef.
Step 5: Demonstrate the Void feature. Show that voided transactions reverse points automatically.
"This works with your existing POS via our API. Three API calls: lookup, transact, redeem. That's it. Every transaction is enriched with store, register, associate, and source data automatically."
Idempotency
If you accidentally submit the same sale twice, the system recognizes the duplicate and returns the original result. No double-points risk, even if the network hiccups.

Act 4 — "Under the Hood" (5 min)

Only cover this section if the audience includes technical stakeholders (CTO, IT Director, engineering leads).

Step 1: Open Swagger UI. Show the full REST API.

Say: "Every feature you just saw is available through this API. Your POS, e-commerce site, and mobile app all integrate here."

Step 2: Open the Developer Docs. Show API reference, SDK documentation, and webhook guides.
Step 3: Show the Architecture page. Highlight multi-tenancy and data isolation.

Say: "Azure-native, multi-tenant architecture. Every Daiso store gets its own isolated database — no data leaks between tenants. Built on Azure Container Apps, Service Bus, and Cosmos DB."

  • Data Isolation: "Every Daiso store gets its own isolated database — no data leaks between tenants."
  • Easy POS Integration: "Works with your existing POS — just 3 API calls: lookup, transact, redeem."
  • No App Required: "Customers don't need to download an app — the member portal is web-based and works on any device."
  • Financial Compliance: "Finance gets ASC 606 compliant liability reporting out of the box."
  • Marketing Automation: "Marketing can connect ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo and trigger campaigns on loyalty events."
  • Flexible Offers: "Visit-based offers with configurable qualification — minimum spend, minimum items, specific stores. Plus free item giveaways."
  • Per-Visit Earn Mode: "Two earn modes — per-dollar for traditional retail, per-visit for high-frequency low-ticket stores like Daiso. Switch with one click."
  • Self-Checkout Ready: "Same loyalty integration at self-checkout — no extra work. Branded kiosk experience at loyaltyselfcheckout."
  • Transaction Enrichment: "Every transaction records store, register, associate, source system, and order reference. Full traceability for finance and ops."
  • Three Report Tabs: "Finance, Marketing, and Operations. Liability, engagement funnels, visit conversion rates — all in one place."
  • Built-in Security: "Fraud detection is built in — velocity checks, location tracking, balance drain detection."

4. FAQ / Objection Handling

Anticipated questions from executives and prepared responses:

QuestionRecommended Answer
"Can we brand it with Daiso colors?" "Already done — every customer touchpoint uses your brand colors, logo, and typography. The platform is white-labeled."
"How long to integrate with our POS?" "Three API calls. We have SDKs for Shopify and WooCommerce. A custom POS integration typically takes 1–2 weeks."
"What about our existing customer data?" "Bulk import via API. We can migrate your existing customer data in a few days, including historical purchase data."
"How much does it cost?" "Pricing is based on active member count. The platform starts free for up to 1,000 members."
"Is it GDPR/CCPA compliant?" "Yes — we support data deletion, export, consent management, and PII encryption at rest. Full audit trail of all data access."
"Can we do birthday rewards?" "Yes — configurable bonus point triggers including birthday, referral, enrollment anniversary, and custom events."
"What if our internet goes down?" "The POS queues transactions locally and submits them when connectivity is restored. Idempotent design means no double-points risk."
"Can we see it work with real data?" "Everything you've seen today is running against a live API with real data. We can load your actual product catalog for a proof-of-concept."
"How do we get started?" "We provision your tenant, import your products, and configure your program. You can be live within 2 weeks."
"How does per-visit mode work?" "Instead of points-per-dollar, every qualifying visit earns a flat number of points. For example, every visit where you spend $5 or more earns 10 points. It's simpler for customers to understand and more predictable for finance to forecast. You set the minimum spend threshold and the points-per-visit amount in Program Config. You can also cap the number of qualifying visits per day to prevent gaming."
"Can we configure what counts as a visit?" "Absolutely. A qualifying visit is defined by configurable rules: minimum spend amount, minimum number of items, qualifying channels (in-store, online, self-checkout), and qualifying stores. You can also choose whether visits are counted per transaction or per day (so a customer who makes two purchases in one day only counts as one visit). All of this is configured in the offer qualification rules."
"Do you support self-checkout?" "Yes. We have a dedicated self-checkout kiosk app at loyaltyselfcheckout.z13.web.core.windows.net. It runs the same loyalty integration as your staffed registers — phone keypad entry, item scanning, offer display, and payment. No extra integration work needed."
"What reporting do we get?" "Three full report tabs. Finance: points liability (ASC 606), breakage estimates, redemption reserve, revenue attribution. Marketing: engagement funnel, at-risk members, tier distribution, offer performance with ROI. Operations: qualified visit analytics, visit conversion rate (% of transactions that qualify), daily visit trends, and program health score. Everything exports to CSV and the API supports Power BI integration."
